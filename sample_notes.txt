Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare food using sunlight, water and carbon dioxide. It takes place in chloroplasts, which contain the green pigment chlorophyll.

Light Reaction

The light reaction occurs in the thylakoid membranes. Light energy splits water molecules, releasing oxygen and producing ATP and NADPH.

Dark Reaction

The dark reaction, also called the Calvin cycle, occurs in the stroma. ATP and NADPH are used to convert carbon dioxide into glucose.

Respiration

Respiration is the process in which cells break down glucose to release energy in the form of ATP. Aerobic respiration needs oxygen and happens mainly in mitochondria.
